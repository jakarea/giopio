## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a good checkout conversion rate?

Industry averages:
- **Top performers:** 25-30%
- **Good:** 15-20%
- **Average:** 10-15%
- **Needs improvement:** Under 10%

### How many checkout steps is ideal?

**Best practice:** 1-3 steps
- Step 1: Shipping information
- Step 2: Payment & billing
- Step 3: Review & confirm

Single-page checkouts also perform well when designed properly.

### Should I offer free shipping?

**Free shipping strategies:**
- **Always free** – Above average order value ($50+)
- **Conditional free** – Minimum purchase amount
- **Flat rate** – Predictable costs
- **Real-time rates** – Actual carrier costs

Free shipping increases cart value by 30-50% on average.

### How do I reduce checkout abandonment?

1. **Show all costs upfront** – No surprises
2. **Enable guest checkout** – No forced accounts
3. **Offer multiple payments** – Customer choice
4. **Optimize for speed** – Under 3 seconds
5. **Build trust** – Security, social proof
6. **Support multiple devices** – Mobile-first
7. **Provide assistance** – Live chat, FAQs
8. **Send abandonment emails** – Recover lost sales
9. **Simplify forms** – Only essential fields
10. **Test continuously** – A/B test improvements

### What is checkout abandonment email?

Automated emails sent to users who start but don't complete checkout:

**Timing:**
- 1 hour after abandonment
- 24 hours later
- 48-72 hours later

**Content:**
- Cart reminder
- Product images
- Social proof
- Incentive (discount, free shipping)
- Easy checkout return link

**Recovery rate:** 5-10% of abandoned carts
