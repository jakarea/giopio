## Weakness of Will: Avoiding UX Pitfalls

### Same as Saying Through Shrinking

**"Weakness of will, which is the same as saying through shrinking from toil and pain"**

Common UX mistakes that create pain points:

1. **Complex navigation** – Users can't find what they need
2. **Slow performance** – Frustrating load times
3. **Confusing layouts** – No clear visual hierarchy
4. **Small touch targets** – Hard to use on mobile
5. **Poor error handling** – Users don't understand what went wrong

## Pain Avoided: But in Certain Matters

### Creating Pleasurable Moments

**"Matters to this principle of selection: he rejects pleasures to secure other greater pleasures"**

Where UX pleasure frequently occurs:

1. **Instant feedback** – Immediate response to actions
2. **Smooth animations** – Delightful transitions
3. **Personalization** – Feels made for them
4. **Surprise & delight** – Unexpected nice touches
5. **Effortless completion** – Tasks feel easy
