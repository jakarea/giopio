## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the legal requirements for a business website?

Essential requirements include privacy policy, terms of service, cookie consent (GDPR), accessibility compliance (WCAG), and clear contact information. Specific requirements vary by industry and location.

### How often should I update my website?

Aim for regular content updates (at least monthly) and immediate updates for security patches. Major redesigns every 2-3 years keep your site modern and competitive.

### Do I need a custom website or can I use templates?

Templates work for basic needs, but custom development offers unique branding, specific functionality, better performance, and scalability. Consider your budget, timeline, and long-term goals.

### What is the cost of maintaining a website?

Costs vary widely: hosting ($5-100/month), domain ($10-15/year), SSL certificate ($0-200/year), maintenance services ($50-500/month), and ongoing updates/optimizations.

### How long does website development take?

Simple sites: 2-4 weeks
E-commerce sites: 6-12 weeks
Custom web applications: 3-6 months
Timelines depend on complexity, revisions, and resource availability.
