## Treatise on the Theory of Ethics: Ethical Web Design

### Building Trust Through Transparency

**"Treatise on the theory of ethics, very popular during the Renaissance 500s"**

Your website should follow ethical design principles:

#### Transparency Ethics:

✅ **Be Honest About:**
- Pricing – No hidden fees
- Business practices – Clear policies
- Data usage – Privacy policy
- Company information – Who you are
- Limitations – What you can/can't do
- Contact methods – How to reach you

❌ **Avoid:**
- Fake testimonials
- Stock photos of "team"
- Vague promises
- Hidden costs
- Deceptive urgency
- Fake scarcity
- Misleading claims

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I make my website look more trustworthy?

1. **Use real photos** – Team, office, products
2. **Show contact info** – Address, phone, email
3. **Add testimonials** – Customer reviews with photos
4. **Keep content fresh** – Recent updates
5. **Fix all errors** – Broken links, typos
6. **Add trust badges** – Security, certifications
7. **Show social proof** – Client logos, follower counts
8. **Be transparent** – Pricing, policies, team
9. **Professional design** – Modern, clean layout
10. **Fast performance** – Speed = professionalism

### What makes a website look unprofessional?

❌ **Major red flags:**
- Stock photos of "team"
- No physical address
- @gmail.com email
- Broken links/errors
- Outdated copyright
- Fake reviews
- Poor grammar/typos
- 90s design aesthetic
- Slow loading
- Not mobile-friendly
- No SSL certificate
- Vague promises
- No contact info
- Fake urgency tactics

### How important is website design for trust?

Extremely important. Research shows:
- **75 milliseconds** – Time to form credibility judgment
- **94%** – First impressions relate to design
- **48%** – Judge credibility by website design
- **57%** – Won't recommend unprofessional site

Good design = immediate trust

### Should I include pricing on my website?

Yes, transparency builds trust:
- **Starting prices** – At least ranges
- **Package options** – Good, better, best
- **What's included** – Clear deliverables
- **No hidden fees** – Be upfront
- **Payment terms** – How/when to pay
- **Refund policy** – Money-back guarantee
